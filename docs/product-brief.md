# LIFEGRAPH AI — Product Brief

**Tagline:** Your Life. Your Intelligence. Your AI.
**Track:** 3 — Know Thyself: Your Data. Your Intelligence.

## Vision
A personal AI operating system that understands, organizes and optimizes a user's life using authorized data from calendars, email, tasks, finances, devices and the environment — minimizing manual planning and decision fatigue while keeping the user in control.

## Core philosophy
```
OBSERVE → UNDERSTAND → ANALYZE → PREDICT → RECOMMEND → EXECUTE
```
Zero-input intelligence: collect what is already available, extract from documents, and ask only the questions that materially affect planning.

## Six domains
1. **Academic** — schedule, assignments, deadlines, attendance, study patterns.
2. **Productivity** — time allocation, completion, peak hours, deadline risk.
3. **Financial** — expenses, budgets, subscriptions, savings, simulations.
4. **Digital** — screen time, app usage, distraction patterns.
5. **Environmental** — weather, air quality, commute, workspace.
6. **Social** — events, conflicts, work-life balance, reminders.

## Signature capabilities
- Central conversational AI assistant (voice + text).
- Smart schedule + free-time optimizer that recommends what to do with open time.
- Intelligent to-do manager with PDF/email extraction and adaptive follow-up questions.
- Preference learning with user-controlled memory.
- Cross-domain relationship graph and what-if simulation engine.
- Consent-based autonomous execution with three autonomy levels.

## 24-hour MVP scope
**P0:** assistant, schedule, tasks, natural-language creation, adaptive questions, PDF extraction, free-time detection, prioritization, recommendations, approval-based execution.
**P1:** preference profile, six-domain dashboard, cross-domain insights, what-if simulation, weekly report.
**P2:** full integrations, background sync, advanced models, wearables.

## Success criteria for the pitch
- Clear, plain-language explanation of the problem in the first 30 seconds.
- One end-to-end demo: PDF → task → free slot → recommendation → approved calendar actions.
- Explicit evidence of privacy, consent and correlation-vs-causation discipline.
